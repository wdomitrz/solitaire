Tap a card to pick it up, then tap where it should go. Drag works too. Arrow keys move around the board, Enter picks up or drops, U undoes, H hints and D turns the stock.

Game details

How to play

Build each column downwards in alternating colours, and move aces to the foundations, then upwards in suit. An empty column takes a king. Turn the stock to find more cards; when it is empty, turn the waste back over if the deal allows it. Tap a card and then its destination, drag it, or double-tap to send it home.
